Cannabinoids Explained

THC, CBD, CBN, CBG & More

Understand how active compounds interact with your body’s endocannabinoid system:

  • THC: Primary psychoactive compound responsible for euphoria.
  • CBD: Non-intoxicating compound valued for relaxation and wellness.
  • CBN: Minor cannabinoid popular for nighttime routines.
  • CBG: Known as the "mother cannabinoid," great for daytime focus.

Terpenes & Aromas

The Entourage Effect

Terpenes are aromatic compounds that give strains their distinct smells and shape overall effects:

Myrcene: Herbal & relaxing
Limonene: Citrusy & mood-boosting
Caryophyllene: Peppery & soothing
Pinene: Pine & mental clarity

Indica vs. Sativa vs. Hybrid

Finding Your Ideal Match
Indica: Often associated with relaxing body effects ("In-Da-Couch").
Sativa: Commonly linked to uplifting, energetic, and cerebral experiences.
Hybrid: Balanced blends engineered for tailored combinations of effects.

Dosing Guide

Start Low, Go Slow

A quick reference guide for edible dosing:

Dose Level Expected Effect
1 – 2.5 mg Microdose Mild relief, focus
3 – 5 mg Low Light euphoria, relaxing
10+ mg Standard Strong effect, high tolerance

Types of Concentrates

Extracts & Textures

Learn the differences between popular solvent-based and solventless extracts:

Live Rosin: Solventless extract made using only ice, water, heat, and pressure to preserve maximum terpenes.
Shatter: Glass-like consistency, known for high stability and potent THC levels.
Wax / Budder: Creamy, easy-to-handle textures perfect for precise dabbing.
Live Resin: Extracted from flash-frozen plant material to preserve fresh plant aromas.
